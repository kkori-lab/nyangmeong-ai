The FIRST image is a style reference sheet of several chibi pixel animals. The
LAST image is a photo of a new dog.
Draw ONE 2x2 sheet of the dog from the LAST image, in exactly the art style of
the FIRST image. TOP row = SITTING: left FRONT view, right side view facing LEFT.
BOTTOM row = STANDING on all four legs: left FRONT view, right side view facing
LEFT. Exactly four figures of the same dog, nothing else.

OUTLINE FIRST - the most important style rule:
Every figure is enclosed by a THICK BLACK OUTLINE, two to three pixels wide, pure
black, unbroken all the way around the silhouette - around the ears, the head,
every leg, every paw and the tail. A figure without a full black outline is
wrong, no matter how fluffy the dog is.

Copy only the art STYLE of the reference - never its animals, colors or layout.

VIEW LOCK
- Four panels in a 2x2 layout. Top row: the dog SITTING. Bottom row: the dog
  STANDING squarely on all four legs. Left column: FRONT view. Right column:
  side view facing LEFT.
- Same dog in all four panels: identical fur colors, markings, ear shape and
  pixel scale. Figures in the same row stand on the same floor line.
- Leave clear empty background around every figure. No figure may touch or cross
  the middle of the canvas, horizontally or vertically, and none may touch the
  canvas edges.
- Left column: the dog faces the viewer, both eyes visible, body symmetric.
- Right column: the dog faces LEFT in profile, exactly ONE eye visible, nose
  pointing left. If both eyes are visible in the right column, that panel is
  wrong.
- Do not reproduce the grid of the reference sheet. Exactly four figures, never
  more, no dividing lines between panels.

STYLE
- Thick black outline, one heavy pixel line, closed all the way around each
  figure with no gaps. The outline must be dark and bold, never faint or broken.
- Big chunky pixels, low resolution look, crisp hard pixel edges, no
  anti-aliasing, no blur.
- 4 to 6 flat colors per figure, almost no gradients, no fur strands.
- Ignore the fur TEXTURE of the photo: no fur strands, no shading streaks, no
  fluffy texture - flat color areas only. Take only the colors and markings.
- Chibi proportions in ALL four figures, standing ones included: the head is as
  large as the whole body, the body is small and round, the legs are short
  stubby nubs. Do not draw realistic dog proportions or long legs.
- Simple face: pink blush on the cheeks, small happy smile.
{eyes}

Keep the dog's own fur colors, markings and ear shape faithful to the photo.
Do not copy the fur color of the reference animals.

No collar, no clothing, no accessories, no lettering, no logo.

Flat solid pure magenta (#FF00FF) background covering the entire canvas edge to
edge, one color only, no border, no gradient, no shadow, no ground line, no text.
Never use magenta on the animal or the item.
